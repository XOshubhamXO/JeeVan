| **Vercel** | Hobby | 100GB bandwidth/mo, 12 serverless functions | Current |

- **Unlimited bandwidth** vs Vercel's 100GB cap
- Global CDN (330+ cities) vs Vercel (100+)
- Free analytics built in
- Zero-config Next.js support via `@cloudflare/next-on-pages`
- Custom domain with free SSL

**Switch if** you exceed Vercel's 100GB/month. For now, Vercel is fine.
