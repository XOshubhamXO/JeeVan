## Full Description (4000 chars)

**JeeVan** bridges advanced software engineering with sustainable natural agriculture — bringing global farming intelligence to your fingertips.

### 🌱 What JeeVan Offers

**Global Plant & Crop Directory**
Browse 10 categories spanning staple crops, heirloom vegetables, exotic fruits, medicinal herbs, fungi, beneficial microbes, algae, and rare non-GMO varieties. Each entry includes botanical names, optimal climate conditions, soil requirements, and growth cycles.

**AI-Powered Crop Advisory**
Get personalized planting recommendations based on your exact location, soil type, rainfall patterns, and seasonal timing. Our AI agent analyzes climate data to suggest the best crops for your land.

**Real-Time Market Rates**
Track live commodity prices from Agmarknet (Indian mandis) and global markets. Monitor price trends for grains, vegetables, fruits, and spices to make informed selling decisions.

**Integrated Ventures Hub**
- **JeeVan Plant Nursery:** Order saplings, indigenous seeds, and rare varieties
- **Gardening Services:** Urban rooftop gardens, composting setups, tool rentals
- **Tech Consulting:** Custom software, web apps, PC builds, startup advisory
- **Creative Media Studio:** Photography, video production, content creation

**Community & Transparency**
Clear labeling differentiates 100% verified chemical-free produce grown at JeeVan Farms (Nalanda, Bihar) from partner-sourced produce via MFPCL network. Support social causes like Pedal4Planet (zero-emission transit) and Adira Biocycle (waste recycling).

### 🌍 For Everyone, Everywhere
Select from 195 countries to unlock localized content in your native language. Dynamic nature visuals adapt to your local weather, season, and daylight — creating an immersive experience that connects you to the land.

### 🔒 Built with Integrity
Dual-tier admin controls with full audit trails. Open-source technology stack with zero recurring software costs. No data tracking beyond what you choose to share.
