## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Information You Provide
- **Onboarding Survey:** Name, age range, country, language preference, and interest category (Natural Produce, Nursery Plants, Tech Consulting, Partnerships, Social Causes).
- **Feedback & Testimonials:** Ratings, reviews, and suggestions you submit voluntarily.

### B. Information Collected Automatically
- **Location Data:** Approximate location (city/region/country) derived from your IP address to show relevant crop recommendations and market rates. With your permission, precise GPS location for weather and soil data.
- **Usage Analytics:** Session duration, pages visited, feature interactions (via Umami Analytics — self-hosted; no third-party tracking).
- **Device Information:** Browser type, operating system, screen resolution (for responsive design optimization).

---

## 3. What We DO NOT Collect

- ❌ Health or biometric data
- ❌ Financial information or payment details
- ❌ Personal contacts or address books
- ❌ Photos, videos, or audio recordings
- ❌ Browsing history from other sites
- ❌ Device identifiers (UDID, IMEI, MAC address)

---

## 4. How We Use Your Information

- **Personalization:** Language selection, theme preferences, localized crop recommendations.
- **Improvement:** Understanding which features are used most to guide development.
- **Communication:** Responding to feedback and inquiries (only if you provide contact details).

We **never** sell your data to third parties.

---

## 5. Data Storage & Security

- Data is stored in PostgreSQL databases with Row-Level Security (RLS).
- Admin access uses bcrypt-hashed passkeys with rate limiting.
- All connections use HTTPS/TLS encryption.
- Telemetry data older than 90 days is automatically anonymized.
